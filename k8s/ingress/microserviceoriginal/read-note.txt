In this folder the original "song" and "resource" microservices deployment resides:
k8s-experimental-service-with-statefulset.yaml
This microservice deployment-file is obtained from previous k8s-exercises that preceded this helm-based exercise.

Base on it helm-templates were elaborated for "song" and audio-"resource" microservices,
which now resides in separate directories:
- the song microservice template - charts/templates/microservice/song
- the audio-resource microservice (former resource microservice) template = charts/templates/microservice/audioresource

Hence, this original deployment could be used to fix potential miss-configuration bugs in templates
and to perform viable microservice deployment in a cluster for deployment completeness microservice + database.
